MSOE Dining Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

MSOE Dining is an independent dining-menu app maintained by the developer of the cc_app project. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Dietary preferences, section customization, and cached menus are stored locally on your device. Your dietary preferences and customization settings are not sent to the developer. Removing the app using iOS's Delete App option removes its local settings and cached menus from that device.

Menu downloads

The app downloads shared menu information from GitHub Pages. GitHub processes these requests and logs IP addresses for security purposes. Dietary selections are applied on your device and are not included in menu requests. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for its data-handling and retention practices.

Optional support

If you submit a GitHub issue, the developer can see your GitHub username and anything you include in the report. Issues in the public repository are publicly visible. Please do not include sensitive personal information. Information voluntarily submitted through support is used to respond to questions and troubleshoot problems. It remains on GitHub unless edited or removed under GitHub's features and policies; closing an issue does not delete it.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, visit the project's support page. For requests involving sensitive information, do not post that information publicly; ask for an appropriate contact method first.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
